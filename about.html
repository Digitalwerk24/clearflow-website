Why ClearFlow

A bridge between convenience and health

The microplastic crisis is real. Our drinking water — including bottled water you trust — is contaminated with particles invisible to the human eye, but proven to enter the bloodstream and accumulate in tissue.

ClearFlow exists because everyone deserves access to cleaner water — without changing their habits, without expensive subscriptions, without specialized equipment.
